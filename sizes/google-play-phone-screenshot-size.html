Google Play phone screenshot size: 1080 x 1920 px (2026)

Google Play accepts screenshots between 320 and 3840 pixels per side, with the long side at most 2x the short side, in JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha. 1080 x 1920 (9:16 portrait) is the safe, standard choice for phones.
