Free "Big Type" app store screenshot template

A complete look for your App Store and Google Play listings: Big Type pairs a oversized captions readable at thumbnail size background with a clean device frame and a bold, readable caption. Best for: undefined.

What the template includes

  • Background gradient + typography tuned for thumbnail-size readability
  • Device frame with dynamic island (iPhone) or clean bezel (Android)
  • Works across every store size: iPhone 6.9"/6.5", iPad 13", Play phone, 7"/10" tablets, feature graphic
  • Localization: re-render captions per language from a CSV (Pro)

How to use it

  1. Open the editor with this template preloaded.
  2. Drop your raw screenshots in.
  3. Type your captions and export - free (watermarked) or Pro ($29 one-time, every size).

Like all StoreShelf templates, it renders deterministically on your machine - no AI image editing quietly mangling your text, and nothing uploaded.
